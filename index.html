Why an armored joint system

Slab edge protection

Designed to protect concrete slab edges against impact and wear.

Central dowel plate

A 5 mm thick central dowel plate forms part of the system.

Anchored in the concrete

Heavy-duty concrete anchor hooks/studs hold the joint in the slab.

Accommodates movement

Designed to accommodate natural concrete movement and help protect joint edges from cracking and deterioration.

Our product

JUBIA Armor Joint – AJ Type
